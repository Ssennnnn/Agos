Emergency

When the waters rise, families are forced to leave.

The Philippines continues to experience flooding brought by the Southwest Monsoon (Habagat) and recent tropical cyclones. Heavy rainfall has caused flooding and evacuations in several parts of Luzon, affecting homes, roads, schools, and livelihoods.

As of August 29, 2026, the Office of Civil Defense reported that around 8 million people or approximately 2.3 million families have been affected by the combined effects of Habagat and recent tropical cyclones.

Last updated: August 29, 2026
Sources: Office of Civil Defense (OCD), PAGASA, local government advisories
