Flood Safety Guide

Know the warnings. Protect your family. Stay safe before, during and after a flood.

Evacuate Immediately

Before the Flood

  1. Know your riskCheck if your home is in a flood-prone area and learn the nearest evacuation center and safe routes.
  2. Prepare your go-bagPack water, food, medicines, a flashlight and copies of important documents in a waterproof bag.
  3. Make a family planAgree on a meeting place and who to call, and make sure everyone knows the plan.
  4. Stay informedFollow PAGASA bulletins and advisories from your local government.

During the Flood

  1. Move to higher groundLeave early when told to evacuate. Do not wait for the water to reach your door.
  2. Avoid floodwaterNever walk, swim or drive through flooded roads. Even shallow, fast water can be dangerous.
  3. Turn off utilitiesIf it is safe, switch off electricity at the main and unplug appliances.
  4. Keep in touchSave phone battery, send short messages, and call emergency lines only for real emergencies.

After the Flood

  1. Wait for the all-clearReturn home only when authorities say it is safe.
  2. Watch for hazardsBeware of weak structures, broken glass, fallen wires and slippery mud.
  3. Protect your healthWear boots and gloves, boil or treat drinking water, and avoid contact with floodwater.
  4. Check your homeTake photos for records and have wiring and gas checked before using them.
  5. Ask for helpContact your barangay or local disaster office for relief and recovery assistance.

What's in your Emergency Kit?

  • Water

    At least 3 liters per person per day

  • Food

    Ready-to-eat, non-perishable items

  • Flashlight

    With extra batteries

  • First Aid Kit

    Bandages, antiseptic and basic supplies

  • Whistle

    To signal for help

  • Medicines

    Personal prescriptions and basics

  • Important Documents

    IDs and papers in a waterproof bag

  • Power Bank

    Fully charged, with cables

  • Extra Clothes

    Dry clothing and a rain jacket

  • Cash

    Small bills and coins
