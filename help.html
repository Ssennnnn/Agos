How You Can Help

Together, we can help communities recover.

Every act of kindness matters. Whether you give, volunteer or simply share accurate information, you can help families rebuild their homes and their lives.

Donate

Support Relief Efforts

Your donation helps provide food, clean water, shelter and medical aid to families who have lost their homes. Give only through accredited organizations such as the Philippine Red Cross or the DSWD.

What your support can provide

  • Food packs and clean drinking water
  • Hygiene kits
  • Emergency shelter
  • Medical supplies
  • Recovery assistance

Donate Essentials

Give What Families Need

Relief goods are collected and repacked by local government units and relief groups. Check with them first so that what you give is what is needed.

Commonly Needed Items

  • Canned goods and rice
  • Bottled water
  • Blankets and mats
  • Clothing
  • Hygiene items and medicines

Volunteer

Give Your Time

Volunteers help pack goods, staff evacuation centers and assist in cleanup. Contact your barangay or a recognized relief group to sign up.

Pre-Training Tips

  • Register with a recognized organization
  • Bring ID, water and protective gear
  • Follow instructions from coordinators
  • Never enter floodwater without proper training

Share Information

Help Keep People Informed

Accurate information saves lives. Share verified advisories so people can prepare and evacuate on time.

How

  • Share official PAGASA and OCD updates
  • Check sources before posting
  • Report people who need rescue to local authorities
  • Avoid spreading rumors or old photos
